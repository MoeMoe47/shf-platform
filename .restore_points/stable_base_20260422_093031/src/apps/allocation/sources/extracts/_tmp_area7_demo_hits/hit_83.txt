                                     WIOA Primary Performance Measures

For all WIOA Core programs: Adult, Dislocated Worker, Youth, Adult Education and Literacy (ASPIRE), Vocational Rehabilitation and Wagner-
Peyser (Wagner-Peyser will be excluded from performance measures 4 and 5):


1) Employment 2nd quarter after exit: The percentage of program participants who are in unsubsidized employment during the second quarter
after exit from the program.


        For CCMEP WIOA Youth: Education, training, or employment 2nd quarter after exit: The percentage of program participants who are
        in education or training activities, or in unsubsidized employment, during the second quarter after exit from the program.


2) Employment 4th quarter after exit: The percentage of program participants who are in unsubsidized employment during the fourth quarter
after exit from the program.


        For CCMEP WIOA Youth: Education, training, or employment 4th quarter after exit: The percentage of program participants who are
        in education or training activities, or in unsubsidized employment, during the fourth quarter after exit from the program.




                                                                                                                                            7
                          WIOA Primary Performance Measures (continued)

3) Median earnings 2nd quarter after exit: The median earnings of program participants who are in unsubsidized employment during the
second quarter after exit from the program.


4) Credential attainment rate: The percentage of program participants who obtain a recognized postsecondary credential or a secondary school
diploma or its recognized equivalent during participation in or within 1 year after exit from the program. Program participants who obtain a
secondary school diploma or its recognized equivalent shall be included in the percentage counted as meeting the criterion if such participants,
in addition to obtaining such diploma or its recognized equivalent, have obtained or retained employment or are in an education or training
program leading to a recognized postsecondary credential within 1 year after exit from the program.


5) Measurable skill gains: The percentage of program participants who, during a program year, are in an education or training program that
leads to a High School Diploma or its Equivalency, a recognized postsecondary credential or employment and who are achieving documented
academic, technical, occupational, or other forms of progress, towards such a credential or employment. Gains will be based on attainment of
one of the following: 1) gain in at least one educational functioning level; 2) High School Diploma or its Equivalency; 3) secondary/post-
secondary transcript/report card showing that the participant is achieving the state unit’s policies for academic standards; 4) satisfactory or
better progress report towards established milestones from the employer/training provider who is providing training; or 5) successful
completion of an exam that is required for a particular occupation, or progress in attaining technical or occupational skills as evidenced by trade-
related benchmarks such as knowledge-based exams.

                  WIOA Methodology for Assessing Performance as detailed
                                              in Ohio Combined State Plan
Under WIOA, official performance evaluation only occurs annually. After the end of the program year, an adjustment of an area’s performance
standard(s) may occur as a result of updated demographic and economic indicators by means of the Statistical Adjustment Model. Only after this
adjustment process is complete can an official evaluation of an area’s performance be accomplished (TEGL 9-17).


Performance on an individual measure will be interpreted based on the position of the outcome relative to the local area performance standard
using the criteria defined below. Achieving a ‘Success’ on all individual measures indicates that a local workforce area performed successfully.
Obtaining a ‘Fails’ on any individual measure constitutes local workforce area failure.


        Success: When the actual local area performance achieved against an individual performance measure falls in the range of 90 percent
        or greater of the performance standard for the measure


        Fails: When the actual local area performance achieved against an individual performance measure is less than 90 percent of the
        performance standard
