# Draft ODOT Rights Clarification Request

This is a draft only. It has not been sent.

## Dataset

Ohio Department of Transportation TIMS County layer, FeatureServer item
`5a65fb89de864e0b97bbcb346dd761f3`:

`https://tims.dot.state.oh.us/ags/rest/services/Boundaries/County/FeatureServer/0`

## Questions

Could ODOT confirm whether Silicon Heartland may:

1. retain a local snapshot for reproducibility and audit;
2. process the export for validation and application rendering;
3. display derived county geometry in SHS applications;
4. retain superseded snapshots after annual updates;
5. redistribute raw or derived geometry, if a public application requires it;
6. use the data in SHS commercial or for-profit applications; and
7. satisfy attribution by retaining `ODOT Office of Technical Services`, the
   source URL, and ODOT's accuracy/liability disclaimer in metadata or map
   attribution?

Please identify required notice, attribution wording, modification restrictions,
and distinctions between internal use and public redistribution.
