# GEO-1 Wave 6 Acceptance Gate

Before an additional client is implemented, confirm:

- source publisher and authority are confirmed;
- stable source identity is present;
- coordinate family and space are registered;
- geometry/data provenance and use terms are documented;
- publication and verification owners are separate and explicit;
- the client has a real route and regression surface;
- the adapter consumes approved Spatial results only;
- no domain inference, name guessing, or fallback identity is introduced;
- privacy and public/private boundaries are testable;
- accessibility and non-map equivalents are planned;
- failure is controlled and never silently fabricates data;
- legacy behavior and rollback are preserved until parity acceptance;
- no existing authority, coordinate space, or source asset is silently replaced.
