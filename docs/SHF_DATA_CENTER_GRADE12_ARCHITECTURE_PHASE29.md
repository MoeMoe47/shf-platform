## Capstone and Team Model

The capstone is a fictional Ohio site scenario balancing compute, network, power, cooling, security, reliability, sustainability, workforce access, and community impacts. It uses `MODEL_C_HYBRID`:

- Future generic Project domain owns the integrated team submission.
- Generic Prepare/Prove owns each learner's individual result, evidence, review, and reassessment.
- Team success never marks every member competent.
- Roles may be duplicated; missing roles are handled through integrated-mode requirements.
- `COLLABORATIVE_MODE` and `INDIVIDUAL_INTEGRATED_MODE` have equivalent learning outcomes.
- A learner can work individually when a six-person team is unavailable.

Team deliverables are an integrated design, dependency/reliability map, operational and incident plan, community/sustainability tradeoff analysis, and final presentation or equivalent. Individual deliverables are role-specific analyses or runbooks. Cross-domain evidence demonstrates dependency reasoning without replacing primary-role proof.

The repository audit found no activated generic youth Project/team submission domain suitable for Phase 29 execution. Therefore no new capstone store or migration is introduced; generic Project integration is a Phase 30 prerequisite before executable capstone work.

## Evidence, Review, and Reassessment

Future advanced proof uses `Activity Result -> Evidence -> Authorized Review -> Competency Decision`, with generic `competency.reviewed` events. One or two advanced branch candidates plus a narrow set of shared integration candidates are sufficient. New evidence may produce a new decision while preserving prior decisions and submission chronology. Optional peer or partner feedback cannot create an institutional competency decision.

## Safety and Accessibility Matrix

All branches retain their Grade 11 safety boundaries. The capstone is simulation or safe-sandbox only. No electrical, HVAC, offensive security, production cloud, or other hazardous professional execution is authorized.

Every future implementation must provide text equivalents for diagrams and telemetry, keyboard and screen-reader support, non-color status, captions/transcripts, asynchronous collaboration, accessible presentation alternatives, and the individual integrated mode. Collaboration accessibility changes the mode, not the technical learning standard.

## Transition and Reporting

Future reporting distinguishes entry, branch participation, capstone submission, evidence, competency decisions, program completion, and transition outcomes. Existing reporting owns `competency.reviewed`; `project.submitted`, `project.reviewed`, and `program.completed` remain future generic event candidates and are not emitted in Phase 29.

Canonical lineage:

`Program -> Grade 12 Eligibility -> Active Specialization Assignment -> Future Grade 12 Course Assignment -> Advanced Lesson Completion -> Activity Result or Project Submission -> Evidence -> Competency Review -> Program Completion -> Transition`

Career Center and Portfolio may later project approved facts but do not own or mutate them. Employers and partners have no default access. Career and transition references remain informational/planning-only.
