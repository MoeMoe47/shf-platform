# SHF Data Center Prepare / Prove Architecture

Phase 15 defines the Grade 11-12 specialization architecture without activating executable branch content.

## Branching Model

The canonical model is `MODEL_B`: Grade 11 begins with a shared core, then a learner selects a primary specialization. Grade 12 deepens that branch and contributes a specialization role to the collaborative `Build Ohio's Next AI Data Center` capstone. Learners may change branches after advising and prerequisite review, add an approved secondary focus through electives, and use an individual equivalent when team work is not accessible.

Six branches are intentionally defined:

1. Data Center Technical Operations
2. Networking and Fiber
3. Electrical Infrastructure
4. Mechanical and HVAC Infrastructure
5. Cybersecurity and Physical Security
6. AI and Cloud Infrastructure

Construction and infrastructure delivery remains an elective and career-exposure track because it supports every branch but does not yet have enough distinct curriculum evidence for a seventh full specialization.

## Shared Core

All Grade 11 learners share safety and professional practice, documentation and technical communication, reliability and systems thinking, evidence and portfolio boundaries, and career and transition planning. The common prerequisite is Grade 10 `data-center-reliable-operations-10`.

## Prepare / Prove Boundary

`PREPARE` means learning, supervised practice, feedback, and artifact creation. `PROVE` means a defined task or assessment may produce reviewable evidence. Neither state is automatically a verified competency. Curriculum owns sequence and content; Activity or Project domains own execution; Evidence owns institutional evidence and review; Credential providers own credential issuance; Career owns canonical career facts; future work-based-learning and employer systems own their explicitly permissioned records.

All future evidence candidates are `PLANNED ONLY`. All credential relationships are `NOT ISSUED / PLANNED`. No competency truth store, credential issuer, employer workflow, readiness score, or partner commitment is created in Phase 15.

## Safety and Privacy

Classroom and simulation work can cover theory, diagrams, synthetic data, and approved supervised labs. Mains electrical work, industrial mechanical work, licensed trade hours, professional equipment, and authorized partner training remain external boundaries. Employer or partner context does not grant access to learner lessons, grades, assessments, portfolio, readiness, or evidence without explicit future authorization.

## Transition

Grade 12 supports multiple destinations: apprenticeship, community college, technical school, university, work-based learning, entry-level employment, and further specialized training. These are options, not placement promises. Career expansion beyond the existing canonical `data-center-technician` record requires a separate Career phase.
