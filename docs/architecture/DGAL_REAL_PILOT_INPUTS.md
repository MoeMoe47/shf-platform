# DGAL Real Pilot Inputs

This document records PR-7 findings for a future DGAL-0 audit. It is not DGAL implementation.

## Onboarding Guidance
- Explain organization review, approval, activation, relationship ownership, and entitlement timing.
- Provide a role-specific first action for admin, operator, ordinary user, reviewer, provider, and learner.

## Printable and Agreement Opportunities
- Organization handoff cover sheet.
- Service entitlement acknowledgment.
- Data-use and source-access acknowledgment.
- Pilot success and escalation checklist.
- Report/evidence acceptance record.

## Tours and Orientation
- Connect first-run orientation to the selected service and role.
- Preserve return/revisit behavior and accessible navigation.
- Make unavailable external providers and safety limits explicit.

## Learning Companion and Smart Prefill
- Link guidance to the canonical Learning Companion where appropriate.
- Prefill organization/service context only from authorized canonical state.
- Never prefill or expose protected evidence, secrets, or another organization’s data.

## Accessibility and Formats
- Provide accessible HTML and printable/PDF variants for operator packets.
- Keep headings, labels, focus order, contrast, text scaling, and reduced-motion behavior intact.

## Pilot Findings
- The repository supports a production-shaped operator-led pilot.
- Real organization users, provider participant UAT, legal approvals, external identity, and production provider activation require outside owners.
- CivicSure provider self-service is now available within provider-scoped canonical authority; DGAL should provide provider role guidance, evidence-submission instructions, correction-response guidance, and accessible printable handoff material.
