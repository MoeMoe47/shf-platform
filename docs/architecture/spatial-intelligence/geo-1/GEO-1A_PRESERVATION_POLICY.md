# GEO-1A Preservation Policy

Preservation before replacement.

No existing map implementation may be deleted merely because a shared Spatial Engine exists.

## Classification Values

- `ACTIVE`
- `ACTIVE_BUT_DEGRADED`
- `DISCONNECTED`
- `UNMOUNTED`
- `ARCHIVED`
- `FROZEN`
- `EXPERIMENTAL`
- `RESTORATION_CANDIDATE`
- `RETIREMENT_CANDIDATE`
- `UNKNOWN`

## Retirement Rule

Retirement requires GEO-10 acceptance evidence. A candidate may be marked for retirement before GEO-10, but deletion or irreversible removal must wait for accepted parity/migration evidence.

## Restoration Rule

Restoration requires an authority owner, route owner, projection boundary, coordinate contract, source-data contract, accessibility plan, and tests appropriate to the surface.

## Historical Material

Locked, frozen, recovery, checkpoint, and disconnected implementations are evidence. They are not canonical merely because they exist, and they are not disposable merely because they are not mounted.
