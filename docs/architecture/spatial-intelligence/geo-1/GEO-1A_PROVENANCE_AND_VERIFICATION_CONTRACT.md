# GEO-1A Provenance and Verification Contract

Every authoritative or public spatial claim should support:

- source authority
- source record
- evidence reference
- verification state
- publication state
- updated timestamp
- freshness/staleness
- public/private eligibility

## Spatial Role

Spatial displays provenance, verification, publication, and freshness metadata supplied by authoritative systems.

## Authority Role

Truth, evidence, reporting, publication, and domain services remain authoritative. Spatial may filter or suppress features according to their states, but it does not approve, verify, publish, or certify claims.

## Public Claim Rule

A public spatial claim requires both an eligible projection and a publication state that allows public display. Coordinates alone are insufficient.
