# GEO-1 Wave 5B Acceptance Gate

Wave 5B is complete only when:

- Wave 5A is frozen;
- Quick Map coverage is reused and remains green;
- Exchange/Capital token and remote-data behavior is protected;
- SHF route and publication suppression are protected;
- IEP county geometry and identifier behavior are protected;
- disconnected globe candidates are inventoried without mounting them;
- known Franklin fallback behavior is documented as a defect, not approved;
- coordinate spaces remain isolated;
- source identity gaps remain explicit;
- publication boundaries remain explicit;
- results are classified as PASS, known condition, or blocker without false-green assertions;
- regression and unexpected failure counts are zero;
- no production adapter, mapping, migration, route fix, resolver fix, or map change occurs;
- prior Spatial, Quick Map, fixture, browser, and build baselines remain green.
