- the map estate registry covers every confirmed client and disconnected candidate;
- all 15 Quick Map entries are audited individually;
- candidate and confirmed authority are separate;
- coordinate family and coordinate-space boundaries are explicit;
- publication and verification owners are separate from source ownership;
- the production source-mapping checklist is defined;
- migration states preserve existing `UNMAPPED` and `PROVISIONAL` meanings;
- known GEO-0 risks are revalidated with repository evidence;
- the Franklin County fallback is recorded as a blocker and remains unfixed in this planning pass;
- SHF publication status, Exchange token dependency, county geometry provenance, and disconnected globes are documented;
- no production adapters, source mappings, migrations, route fixes, or map changes are made;
- current application behavior remains unchanged;
- existing Spatial, Quick Map, fixture, browser, and build baselines pass.
