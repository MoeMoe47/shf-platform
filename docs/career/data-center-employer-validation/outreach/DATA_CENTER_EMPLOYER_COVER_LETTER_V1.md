**Re: Request for professional review — Data Center & AI Infrastructure Career Pathway**

Silicon Heartland Foundation (SHF) is a nonprofit education organization operating a K-12 Career Center. This letter accompanies a package asking for your professional review of one of our programs: a Data Center & AI Infrastructure curriculum for grades 6 through 12.

**Why we're asking, and why now.** SHF recently adopted a formal internal standard — the Career Pathway Acceptance Standard — that governs how we classify and describe our career-preparation programs. That standard requires any program we describe as "employer-validated" or "industry-relevant" to actually be reviewed by real employers or industry experts first. The Data Center & AI Infrastructure pathway is the first program we are running through this process, precisely because it is one of our more developed offerings and a meaningful early test of whether our approach works.

**What "PREPARE" and "NOT REVIEWED" mean.** Under our standard, this pathway is currently classified PREPARE — meaning we have built substantive curriculum, practice activities, and internal assessment, but have not yet connected it to a state-recognized pathway or employer validation. Its validation status is NOT REVIEWED, meaning no external party has yet examined it. We are not describing it as more than that anywhere, and we are asking you to help us determine, honestly, whether it deserves to move forward.

**What your review would mean — and would not mean.** A review from you would help us understand whether this curriculum reflects real entry-level work in your field. It would not commit you or your organization to hiring, an internship or apprenticeship placement, endorsing a credential, or any partnership. Those are separate conversations we would only pursue later, and only if you were interested.

**How we will use your feedback.** Your responses will be recorded and attributed to you and your organization in our internal records. If your review meets the bar our standard sets for it — a defined scope, engagement with the technical detail relevant to your expertise, and no unresolved safety or role-alignment concerns — we may record this pathway as "Employer Reviewed" or, with fuller engagement, "Employer Validated" for the specific competencies you assessed. A single positive comment or a partially completed questionnaire would not be sufficient for that on its own, and we want you to know that up front so there's no ambiguity about what your time is being used for.

We would welcome your participation, but there is no obligation and no pressure attached to this request. If you have questions, concerns, or would simply prefer not to participate, please let us know — either response is useful to us.
