# Data Center & AI Infrastructure — Employer Outreach Email v1

**Template only. Not sent. Requires a real recipient name/organization, and owner approval, before use.**

---

**Subject:** Requesting your professional review of a K-12 Data Center & AI Infrastructure curriculum

Dear [Reviewer Name],

My name is [SHF contact name], and I'm reaching out from Silicon Heartland Foundation (SHF), a nonprofit education organization that runs a K-12 Career Center. I'm writing because of your experience in [data center operations / infrastructure and facilities / workforce and training] — not to ask for a commitment, but to ask for your professional opinion.

SHF has built a curriculum for grades 6 through 12 that introduces students to data center and AI infrastructure work — systems, networking, electrical and mechanical concepts, cybersecurity, and AI/cloud infrastructure. Under SHF's own Career Pathway Acceptance Standard, this program is currently classified **PREPARE** (foundational preparation has been built) and **NOT REVIEWED** (no employer or industry expert has yet reviewed it). We don't want to describe this program as validated, industry-relevant, or a real route toward employment until someone like you has actually looked at it and told us whether it holds up.

That's what we're asking: **a professional review, not an endorsement.** If you're willing, we'll send you a short package describing the program honestly, including what it does well and what it doesn't do yet, along with a questionnaire. The questionnaire has a Core section that should be sufficient on its own, and an Optional Deep Technical section for anyone with the additional time and hands-on expertise to go further.

To be direct about what we are **not** asking for: this is not a request for hiring, an internship or apprenticeship commitment, a credential endorsement, or a partnership of any kind. If, after reviewing, you'd be open to discussing any of those things in the future, we'd welcome that conversation — but it's entirely separate from this request.

Because this program serves students as young as grade 6, we would also value your perspective on age-appropriate exposure to real facility environments — what a minor could safely observe, simulate, or assist with under supervision, versus what should never be part of a student's experience.

If you're open to this, I'll send the full package and questionnaire. There's no deadline pressure — we're looking for substantive, honest feedback, including anything you think should be corrected, removed, or strengthened, whenever you have time to give it.

Thank you for considering this.

[SHF contact name]
[Title]
Silicon Heartland Foundation
