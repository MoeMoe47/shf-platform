## Reviewer Type Distinction (read before deciding)

Not every reviewer's favorable feedback carries the same weight toward the same outcome. Three distinct reviewer types appear in this cohort:

- **EMPLOYER / OPERATOR REVIEWER** — an organization that would actually hire graduates of this pathway and can speak to on-the-job, workplace relevance. Only this type of reviewer's conclusion can support the CPAS-1 standard's specific definition of **EMPLOYER-VALIDATED** ("an external employer or industry reviewer has confirmed the curriculum and competencies are relevant to real entry-level work"). Candidates: AWS, QTS, Vantage, Mid-City Electric.
- **TRAINING / EDUCATION REVIEWER** — an organization whose primary function is teaching or credentialing, not hiring. Their feedback is valuable for curriculum-to-credential alignment and pedagogical design, but a favorable review from this type alone should **not** be recorded as EMPLOYER-VALIDATED — it supports EMPLOYER REVIEWED status and informs future STATE-PATHWAY or credential-strategy decisions instead. Candidates: Columbus State Community College, Eastland-Fairfield.
- **APPRENTICESHIP / WORKFORCE REVIEWER** — a body that bridges training and employment through a formal apprenticeship or workforce-intermediary structure, without being a single hiring employer itself. Valuable for both technical/safety input and future work-based-learning design, but like the training/education type, should not alone be recorded as an employer's validation of workplace relevance. Candidate: IBEW Local 683 / Electrical Trades Center (a JATC/union body, not a single employer, even though its signatory contractors are real employers).

**Practical consequence:** reaching a genuine EMPLOYER-VALIDATED outcome for this pathway requires at least one completed, sufficient review from the Employer/Operator category specifically — reviews from the other two types, however positive, are not a substitute.

## Owner Options (per candidate — choices, not decisions)

For each row above, the available options are:

- **HOLD FOR MORE VERIFICATION** — do not proceed until a specific named gap (see "Main Limitation") is resolved.
