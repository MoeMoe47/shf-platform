Sequences the Tier 1 cohort from [DATA_CENTER_FIRST_COHORT_PRIORITY_LIST_V1.md](./DATA_CENTER_FIRST_COHORT_PRIORITY_LIST_V1.md) into practical waves. **No organization in this plan has been contacted. This is sequencing, not execution — actual outreach requires separate owner approval per the stop condition below.**

**Current pathway status: PREPARE. Current validation status: NOT REVIEWED.** Sequencing a wave plan does not change either.

## Why Not "Biggest Company First"

AWS is the most recognizable name researched, but it also scored **LOW** on Accessibility of Reviewer Type — hyperscale community-affairs processes are typically slow and formal. Leading with a harder-to-reach organization risks a slow or non-response as the cohort's first data point. Instead, Wave 1 leads with three organizations that combine strong relevance with realistically better first-touch accessibility, one from each category, so SHF gets fast, complementary, cross-category feedback before expanding.

## Wave 1 — Complementary Starter Set (3 candidates, one per category)

| Organization | Category | Why Wave 1 |
|---|---|---|
| QTS Data Centers | A | Its own "Data Center Academy" closely mirrors SHF's preparation model, giving an unusually direct comparison point; moderate (not hyperscale-slow) accessibility. |
| IBEW Local 683 / Electrical Trades Center | B | Highest overall fit score in the entire research; real local scale and an existing K-12-to-apprenticeship precedent make this a strong anchor reviewer. |
| Columbus State Community College | C | The clearest curriculum-to-credential bridge, reinforced by an already-documented AWS scholarship connection to the exact program in question. |

**Wave 1 goal:** get one credible, complementary review from each perspective (operator, infrastructure/technical, workforce/education) before deciding how to expand. This is enough to reach EMPLOYER REVIEWED from at least one organization and start real evidence accumulation, without over-committing outreach effort before learning how the process actually goes.

## Wave 3 — Strategic Follow-Up (after initial validation feedback)

Pursued only after Wave 1 and Wave 2 produce real feedback that clarifies what SHF still needs:

- **If technical-specialization gaps emerge** (e.g., AI/cloud infrastructure specifically): approach Google and/or Meta, whose confirmed Ohio workforce investments are large but not yet confirmed to include curriculum-review scope — worth testing once SHF has a track record from Waves 1-2 to point to.
- **If power-systems-specific depth is needed**: approach AEP Ohio, whose lineworker/grid focus is adjacent rather than identical to SHF's content — best used to sanity-check power concepts narrowly, not as a general reviewer.
- **If mechanical/HVAC-specific depth is needed**: approach the strongest of the three regional HVAC contractors (Ohio Heating or Accurate), narrowly scoped to the mechanical/HVAC specialization only.
- **If Tolles's electrical pre-apprenticeship claim is re-verified as real**: reconsider it alongside Eastland-Fairfield for a second K-12 CTE perspective.
- **The Baker Group and IEC Central Ohio remain Tier 3** — pursued only if their Ohio presence/program details are independently confirmed; not part of any wave until then.
- **Cologix** remains a reasonable Wave 3 candidate specifically for its direct "Data Center Technician" title match, useful as a smaller, more directly-reachable comparison point to the larger operators.

## Sequence Within Each Wave (per organization, once approved)

1. Initial email/introduction (ask only whether they're open to reviewing).
2. Send the employer validation package once they confirm interest.
3. Reviewer completes the Core questionnaire (Part 1).
4. Optional Deep Technical review (Part 2), per the [Review Scope Matrix](./DATA_CENTER_REVIEW_SCOPE_MATRIX_V1.md)'s recommended sections for that organization.
5. SHF records feedback in the Employer Feedback Record.
6. SHF determines Employer Reviewed vs. sufficiency for Employer Validated, against the sufficiency rule in the Validation Record.
7. Required corrections are recorded regardless of outcome.
8. Any future partnership or work-based-learning interest is discussed separately, only if raised by the reviewer.
