# Columbus State Community College — Review Scope Document v1

One-page reference for the Columbus State reviewer. Reviewer type: **TRAINING / EDUCATION**.

## Why You

Columbus State runs a real Data Center Technician certificate program — the closest postsecondary parallel to what SHF's K-12 curriculum aims to prepare students toward — with documented industry scholarship support. That makes your program uniquely positioned to assess whether our curriculum is a credible on-ramp toward further technical training, and where the real gaps are.

## What We Would Value Your Review Of

- Curriculum progression across our grade bands (6-8 discovery, 9-10 exploration, 11-12 specialization)
- Whether our technical foundations reasonably prepare a student for further study in a program like yours
- The transition from K-12 preparation into postsecondary technical training generally
- Credential-preparation relevance (what should a student know before pursuing a certificate like yours?)
- Learner readiness and age-appropriate sequencing
- Gaps between SHF's preparation and what a program like Columbus State's would expect from an incoming student
- Potential future alignment areas between SHF's preparation and existing Data Center training (as a topic for discussion, not a proposal)

## What We Are NOT Asking You to Validate

That an articulation agreement exists or should exist; that college credit is or will be awarded for SHF coursework; that automatic or preferential admission exists; that any formal partnership exists; or that Columbus State recognizes or endorses any SHF credential. None of these are proposed, implied, or assumed anywhere in this request.

## Core Questionnaire Sections to Prioritize

C (Curriculum Relevance), I (Credentials), J (Work-Based Learning, framed as future/exploratory only), A (Identity), K (Overall Outcome).

## Optional Deep Technical Sections

**Optional / selective** — this reviewer's value is in curriculum-to-credential alignment, not hands-on technical assessment. Section U (Practical Realism / Role-Specific Skill Gaps) may be useful if a specific faculty member has direct program-design experience relevant to it, but no Deep Technical section is expected.

## Student / Minor Safety Questions Relevant to You

While your program serves adult/postsecondary learners rather than minors directly, we'd value your perspective on age-appropriate sequencing (Questionnaire C2) and any observations on how our grades 6-12 progression maps to the technical maturity your program expects — this speaks to design quality, not facility-safety specifics.

## What Happens With Your Feedback

Recorded in our internal Employer Feedback Record, attributed to Columbus State, with your reviewer type explicitly noted. Your review supports "Employer Reviewed" status and directly informs our curriculum and credential-strategy decisions. It will not, alone, be recorded as an employer's validation of workplace relevance — that requires a hiring-employer perspective, sought separately in this cohort.

## No Commitment / No Partnership Statement

Participating in this review does not create, propose, or imply any articulation agreement, transfer credit, admission pathway, credential recognition, or partnership between SHF and Columbus State. Any future conversation about program alignment would be entirely separate, and only pursued if your institution expressed interest.
