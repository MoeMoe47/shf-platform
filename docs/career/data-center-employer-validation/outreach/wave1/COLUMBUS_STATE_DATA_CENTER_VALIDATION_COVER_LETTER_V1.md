**Re: Request for a curriculum-and-credential review — Data Center & AI Infrastructure Career Pathway**

Silicon Heartland Foundation (SHF) is a nonprofit education organization operating a K-12 Career Center in Columbus. This letter accompanies a package asking for your review of our Data Center & AI Infrastructure curriculum, grades 6-12.

**Why Columbus State.** Your Data Center Technician certificate program is the clearest real postsecondary parallel to what our K-12 curriculum is trying to prepare students toward. Your program's connections to industry — including scholarship support tied to data center employers — give you a grounded, current view of what real technical training and credentialing in this field actually requires, which is exactly the perspective we need on curriculum progression and readiness.

**A clarification we want to make explicitly.** This request is not a proposal for articulation, transfer credit, guaranteed or preferential admission, dual enrollment, or any formal partnership. We are asking for an honest, professional assessment of our curriculum and how it might or might not prepare a student for further study — nothing about your program's structure or relationship to SHF is being proposed or assumed.

**Current status.** This pathway is classified **PREPARE** — real preparation exists but has not been connected to any recognized outside pathway — and **NOT REVIEWED**. We are not describing it as more than that anywhere.

**What your review would mean, and would not mean.** A review from Columbus State would tell us whether our curriculum's progression and technical foundations are a credible starting point toward further training, and where the gaps are. It would not commit your institution to any credit, admission, or credentialing arrangement, nor to any partnership.

**How we will use your feedback.** Your responses will be recorded and attributed to Columbus State in our internal records, with the reviewer-type distinction (a training/education institution, not a hiring employer) preserved explicitly in how we record any outcome — your review supports curriculum and credential-strategy decisions, not a claim that an employer has validated workplace relevance.

We would welcome your participation, with no obligation attached. If this isn't the right fit for your team right now, we would appreciate knowing that too.
