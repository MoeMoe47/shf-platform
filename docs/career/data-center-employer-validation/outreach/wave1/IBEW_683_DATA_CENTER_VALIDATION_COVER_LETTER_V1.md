**Re: Request for an apprenticeship-training perspective — Data Center & AI Infrastructure Career Pathway**

Silicon Heartland Foundation (SHF) is a nonprofit education organization operating a K-12 Career Center in Columbus. This letter accompanies a package asking for your review of the electrical-infrastructure portion of our Data Center & AI Infrastructure curriculum, grades 6-12.

**Why the Electrical Trades Center.** Your apprentices are performing real electrical and teledata work on Central Ohio's data center construction boom today, and your Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee runs a real, substantial Registered Apprenticeship — including, we understand, a student-partner pathway that has let high school students transition directly into second-year apprenticeship standing. That direct, hands-on experience with both the technical content and the real question of what a student can safely be exposed to before formal training makes your perspective uniquely valuable to us.

**A clarification we want to make explicitly.** We are asking the Electrical Trades Center to review our electrical/safety content specifically — not to serve as SHF's sole validator for the entire Data Center pathway, and not as a request related to apprenticeship admission, sponsorship, or placement for any specific student. A separate employer/operator review is being pursued elsewhere in our process for the broader data-center-operations perspective; your review would stand on its own, for the scope it actually covers.

**Current status.** This pathway is classified **PREPARE** under our Career Pathway Acceptance Standard — real preparation exists, but nothing beyond SHF's own classroom has confirmed it — and **NOT REVIEWED**. We are not describing it as more than that anywhere.

**What your review would mean, and would not mean.** A review from you would tell us whether our electrical-infrastructure content, safety boundary (reasoning/simulation only, never real electrical work), and age-appropriateness judgment are sound. It would not commit your organization to accepting any student into an apprenticeship, sponsoring one, or any partnership. Those would be entirely separate conversations, and only if you were interested.

**How we will use your feedback.** Your responses will be recorded and attributed to the Electrical Trades Center / IBEW Local 683 in our internal records, with the reviewer-type distinction (a training/apprenticeship body, not a single hiring employer) preserved explicitly in how we record any outcome.

We would welcome your participation, with no obligation attached. If this isn't the right fit, we would appreciate knowing that too.
