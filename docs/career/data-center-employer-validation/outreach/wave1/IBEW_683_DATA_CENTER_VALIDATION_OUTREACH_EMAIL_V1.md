Dear Electrical Trades Center team,

My name is [SHF contact name], and I'm reaching out from Silicon Heartland Foundation (SHF), a nonprofit education organization running a K-12 Career Center in Columbus. I'm writing because of your organization's real, direct involvement in Central Ohio's data center construction and your formal electrical apprenticeship training program — not to ask for a commitment, but for your professional review.

SHF has built a curriculum for grades 6 through 12 introducing students to data center operations, including an electrical-infrastructure specialization track. Under our internal Career Pathway Acceptance Standard, this program is currently classified **PREPARE** (foundational preparation built) and **NOT REVIEWED** (no employer or training body has yet reviewed it).

**Why the Electrical Trades Center specifically:** your apprentices are doing real electrical and teledata work on Central Ohio data center construction right now, and your Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee has real, direct experience with what a rising student can and cannot safely be exposed to before and during a formal apprenticeship. **What we're asking:** a review focused on electrical fundamentals, physical infrastructure concepts, safety boundaries, PPE, supervision expectations, and — because our curriculum currently only lets students reason about simulated electrical scenarios and never perform real electrical work — whether that boundary is the right one. We would also deeply value your perspective on age-appropriateness and what a real transition from a K-12 program into apprenticeship-level training should look like.

**An important clarification:** we are not asking the Electrical Trades Center to be the sole validator of this entire pathway, and we are not asking about or implying anything regarding automatic apprenticeship entry or apprenticeship sponsorship for our students. Your review would speak specifically to the electrical/safety/training-transition perspective — a separate employer-operator review is being sought elsewhere in our process for the broader operations perspective.

**What we are not asking for:** a hiring commitment, a partnership, or any apprenticeship placement guarantee. If, after reviewing, there were ever interest in exploring how a future work-based-learning route might connect, we would welcome that as a separate conversation.

We'll send a Core questionnaire that should be sufficient on its own, along with the electrical/safety sections of an Optional Deep Technical review we would specifically value your input on.
