## Why You

QTS operates active data center facilities in New Albany, Ohio — in the same region as SHF's students — and runs its own real technical-training model (a "Data Center Academy" toward a Critical Operations Technician role) plus New Albany-specific internship and workforce programs. That combination of local presence and operator-level training experience makes your organization one of the most directly relevant reviewers available to us for the *employer/operator* perspective specifically.

## What We Would Value Your Review Of

- Whether "Data Center Technician" is the right primary target role

- Safety framing and the boundary between simulated reasoning and real work
- Facility access/security concepts as we teach them
- Documentation and escalation practices

## Student / Minor Safety Questions Relevant to You

Because our program serves students as young as grade 6, we would specifically value your input on Questionnaire E3-E8: what areas of a real data center facility could a minor safely observe under supervision, what should never be part of a minor's experience regardless of supervision, and what PPE/supervision protocols your site would require for any future in-person exposure. We are not asking for a legal conclusion — only your organization's actual policy and judgment.

## What Happens With Your Feedback

Your responses are recorded in our internal Employer Feedback Record, attributed to QTS. Reaching an "Employer Reviewed" outcome requires your completed Core questionnaire and identity. Reaching "Employer Validated" for any specific competency requires more — an explicit conclusion from you, a defined scope, and engagement with the relevant Deep Technical sections. A generally positive impression alone does not produce a validated outcome; we want you to know that up front.

Participating in this review does not commit QTS to hiring, an internship or apprenticeship agreement, a credential endorsement, or a partnership of any kind. If, after reviewing, you'd be open to a future conversation about any of those, we would welcome it as an entirely separate discussion.
