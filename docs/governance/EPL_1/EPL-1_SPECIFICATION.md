# EPL-1 Evidence Provenance Ledger Specification

Standard ID: EPL-1

Standard: Evidence Provenance Ledger

Version: 1.0

Status: GOVERNANCE_DEFINED

Canonical owner: Evidence Governance Authority

EPL-1 is the single canonical chain of custody for SHS BOS engineering evidence. Mission, Architecture, Blueprint, Implementation, Testing, Validation, Certification, Publication, and Post-Publication Verification evidence must register through EPL-1.

No engineering artifact may be certified without evidence registered through EPL-1.

EPL-1 records evidence for Semantic Constitution, AEIB-1, ERA-1, IGLS-1, Mission Definition, Architecture Charter, CTB-1, BDL-1, IRS-1, Master Layer Registry, Truth Spine, Oracle, and Executive Command. It does not redefine those authorities.

EPL-1 answers what evidence exists, who created it, why it exists, which authority required it, which validator verified it, which certification accepted it, which publication relied upon it, whether it can be reproduced, whether it can be trusted, and whether it can be superseded.

Implementation, runtime, API, UI, persistence, and duplicate evidence authority are not authorized by this standard artifact.
