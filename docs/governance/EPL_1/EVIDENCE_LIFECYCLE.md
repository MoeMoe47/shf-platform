# EPL-1 Evidence Lifecycle

States: Created, Registered, Validated, Accepted, Certified, Published, Archived, Superseded, and Retired.

Every transition must define entry criteria, exit criteria, approval authority, and evidence required.

Entry criteria require state-specific prior evidence and ownership. Exit criteria require integrity, traceability, and required authority checks. Each transition records a chain-of-custody event, validation result, and repository reference.
