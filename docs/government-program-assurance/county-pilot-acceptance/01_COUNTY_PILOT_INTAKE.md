## Pilot Objective

Validate that a participating county can operate the frozen GPA v1 workflows over authorized county data: funding-to-outcome lineage, Claim/Evidence/Verification, Monitoring/Findings/Corrective Action, Reconciliation/Data Quality, Audit, governed AI, reporting, and public-safe disclosure.

## Pilot Success Criteria

- Authorized sources connect in the approved environment.
- Users and scopes are provisioned with least privilege.
- One end-to-end Funding → Outcome → Truth lineage is verified.
- Claim/Verification, Monitoring, Reconciliation, Audit, AI briefing, and Executive Report scenarios pass.
- Cross-tenant, cross-organization, restricted-data, purpose, and AI action-guard tests pass.
- County technical, security, operational, and executive sign-offs are recorded.
