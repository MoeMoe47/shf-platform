# Silicon Heartland Legal Layer v2.0

## Phase 0 — Legal Evidence Preservation and Canonical Ownership Decisions

This is a bounded, non-operative technical-preparation package. It preserves repository evidence, maps proposed authority boundaries, records unresolved owner/board/counsel questions, and prepares Phase 1. It is not legal advice, legal drafting, board approval, agreement execution, or authorization for external operations.

## Scope

- Preserve legal and legal-adjacent repository evidence without treating technical material as legal authority.
- Map SHF, SHS, programs, network organizations, education, workforce, agent, Treasury and reporting responsibilities.
- Trace G-001 through G-018 to existing technical authorities and future decisions.
- Define secure external custody for executed legal records.
- Prepare owner, board, accounting, insurance and counsel review.

## Explicit exclusions

No operative agreements, agreement templates for signature, Legal Registry, schema, migration, API, runtime enforcement, external integration, database change, authentication change, authorization change, source-code change outside this directory, deployment, pilot authorization or external launch authorization is created. This package does not establish legal ownership, execution, consent, spending authority, custody, data sharing, publication rights or compliance.

Sensitive executed agreements must not be stored in source control. Phase 0 is not institutionally complete until required owner, board, counsel, accounting and insurance decisions are independently recorded.

## Relationship to later phases

Phase 1 is owner/board/counsel-approved SHF/SHS entity and related-party work. Later phases cover agreement lifecycle, network/incubation, privacy/youth/accessibility, AI/ARAG-1, workforce, Treasury/impact, runtime revocation, assurance and independent verification. No later phase is authorized by this package.

## Carried-forward blockers

P0 structural risks: absence of verified SHF/SHS legal separation and related-party controls; absence of canonical executed agreement authority; absence of complete data/IP ownership and consent authority. P1 blockers include network participation, incubation/spinout, privacy/youth, workforce, AI provider, ARAG-1, Treasury, public reporting, insurance, credential reliance and offboarding controls. These are audit findings, not legal conclusions.

## Required institutional participants

SHF board and officers; SHS owner and authorized signers; nonprofit, tax and related-party counsel; privacy/education/youth counsel; employment/workforce counsel; IP/technology counsel; AI and commercial counsel; accounting/finance; insurance/risk; program, curriculum, accessibility, security, reporting and data owners.
