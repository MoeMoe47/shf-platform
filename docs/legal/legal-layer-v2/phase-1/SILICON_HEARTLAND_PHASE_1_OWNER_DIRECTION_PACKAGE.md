## 1. Purpose and limitations

This package records the owner's proposed direction for preparing the Silicon Heartland Foundation (SHF) and Silicon Heartland Solutions (SHS) structure. It supports coordinated professional review and future formation work.

This package:

- is not legal, tax, accounting, or insurance advice;
- is not an operative agreement, board resolution, filing, consent, license, or authorization;
- does not establish that SHF or SHS has been legally formed;
- does not establish tax-exempt status, signing authority, board authority, ownership title, insurance coverage, or permission to operate externally;
- does not authorize programs, fundraising, data sharing, youth activities, employer services, fiscal sponsorship, ARAG-1 customer operations, or treasury activity.
