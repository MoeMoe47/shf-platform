# Silicon Heartland Metaverse
## Living City Visual Rules V1

## Non-Negotiable Design Law
CITY CANVAS = THE WORLD  
SIDEBAR = GLOBAL SYSTEMS  
RIGHT DRAWER = TEMPORARY CONTEXT  
LIVING CITY LAYERS = SUBTLE BACKGROUND LIFE

The city should receive approximately 85-90% of visual attention.

No permanent dashboard clutter.

## Canonical Layout
LEFT:
- slim collapsible sidebar

CENTER:
- open city canvas

TOP CENTER:
- compact Next Action

RIGHT:
- one temporary closable contextual drawer

BOTTOM CENTER:
- compact contextual navigation

BOTTOM RIGHT:
- small Mini Map + camera controls

## Sidebar
Global systems may include:
- Home
- Next Action
- Silicon Heartland Today / Daily Briefing
- Missions
- Opportunities
- Opportunity Exchange
- Student Market
- Work Passport
- Civic
- Student Enterprise
- District Pulse
- Locations / Mini Map
- Settings
- status
- aggregate presence

Requirements:
- collapsible
- narrow by default
- keyboard usable
- mobile/tablet usable
- must not cover most of the city

## Right Context Drawer
May open for:
- district
- facility
- mission
- opportunity
- civic location
- enterprise
- simulation
- event
- building preview

May show:
- preview
- title
- availability
- description
- source-backed activity
- relevant features
- Enter / Fast Travel CTA
- bounded local presence where appropriate

Rules:
- closed by default
- one major drawer at a time
- Escape closes
- focus returns to originating control
- no client-side authority creation

## Clouds
- 1-2 layers preferred
- soft, semi-transparent
- mostly upper sky
- extremely slow drift
- far layer slower than near layer
- no storm look by default
- no heavy animated blur
- static or removed under reduced motion
- lower complexity in mobile/LOW mode

## Birds
- tiny silhouettes
- sparse
- usually 1-3 visible at once
- slow gliding
- mostly sky / river / upper scene
- no cartoon look
- no large flocks
- long gaps where none are visible
- remove or strongly reduce under reduced motion

## Wind Turbines
- very slow rotation
- smooth
- calm infrastructure motion
- perspective-aligned
- reduce/disable under reduced motion when appropriate

## Cars
- tiny dark charcoal rounded rectangles
- no detailed car art
- no headlights
- no taillights
- no glow
- sparse
- slow
- road-trace only

## Buses
- elongated pills
- dark body
- light top
- slightly larger than cars
- rarer
- slower
- approved bus/transit routes only

## Freeway Streaks
- only on approved freeway/bridge corridors
- thin
- low opacity
- restrained
- road-aligned
- no neon/cyberpunk effect

## Perspective
Higher on screen = farther = smaller.  
Lower on screen = nearer = slightly larger.

Scale transition must be smooth.

Near/bottom vehicles should be roughly 1.5x-2x far/top size maximum.

## Time of Day
Supported:
- AUTO
- DAY
- DUSK
- NIGHT

Presentation only.  
Must not change user authority, unlocks, eligibility, or institutional truth.

## Performance / Accessibility
- reduced-motion support required
- mobile/LOW mode reduces density and complexity
- no stacked drawers/sheets
- city remains visible and usable

## Acceptance Principle
The viewer should notice that the city feels alive before noticing the animation system itself.
