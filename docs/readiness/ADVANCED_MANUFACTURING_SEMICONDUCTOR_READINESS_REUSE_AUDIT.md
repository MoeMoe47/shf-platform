# Advanced Manufacturing & Semiconductor Readiness — Reuse Audit

Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1
Branch: agent/readiness-manufacturing
Baseline: 518dda1
Stable key: `shu-advanced-manufacturing-semiconductor-readiness`

## Purpose

Determine what SHU already contains for Advanced Manufacturing & Semiconductor Readiness before creating curriculum, services, registries, schemas, competencies, or authorities.

Required rule:

Audit → Identify Canonical Authority → Reuse → Extend → Build New Only When Proven Missing.

## Executive Finding

SHU contains existing Advanced Manufacturing / CNC pathway concepts and shared workforce infrastructure, but this audit did not prove an existing governed Advanced Manufacturing & Semiconductor Readiness course or semiconductor-specific curriculum authority.

Existing repository evidence includes:

- Advanced Manufacturing Tech (CNC) pathway concepts
- CNC operator references
- quality / QA context
- maintenance context
- metrology references
- manufacturing eligibility in Ohio employer/workforce program data
- canonical SHU curriculum, assignment, evidence, completion, career, accessibility, portfolio, credential, and reporting authorities

The search did not prove an existing semiconductor-specific readiness course, fab/wafer curriculum, cleanroom readiness curriculum, or semiconductor proof configuration.

## Safety Boundary

The course may teach readiness concepts such as:

- hazard recognition
- PPE awareness
- stop-work / escalation
- quality discipline
- machine-area awareness
- cleanroom behavior awareness
- contamination prevention awareness
- safe communication
- verification

The course must not claim that completion authorizes learners to:

- operate industrial machinery unsupervised
- perform electrical maintenance
- bypass safety systems
- perform lockout/tagout independently
- operate production CNC equipment
- enter controlled fab environments
- perform hazardous chemical handling
- perform regulated semiconductor production tasks

Those permissions belong to their owning employer, training, safety, or regulatory authorities.

## Semiconductor Boundary

No semiconductor-specific authority was proven by this audit.

Semiconductor curriculum should be introduced as contextualized readiness unless and until repository or external authority establishes:

- canonical semiconductor career records
- validated industry competencies
- employer / industry partner standards
- approved technical training authority

Do not fabricate competency IDs.
