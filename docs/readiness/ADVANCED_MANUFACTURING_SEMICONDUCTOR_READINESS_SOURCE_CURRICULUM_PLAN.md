## 2. Course Intent

Advanced Manufacturing & Semiconductor Readiness prepares learners to understand modern manufacturing workplaces, production systems, safety culture, quality, measurement, documentation, troubleshooting, automation, maintenance awareness, clean manufacturing, semiconductor environments, career pathways, evidence, and transition to further technical training.

The course is a readiness layer.

It is not a substitute for:

- employer-specific machine qualification
- OSHA-required employer training
- electrical licensing
- industrial maintenance qualification
- CNC operator qualification
- robotics technician qualification
- cleanroom certification
- semiconductor fab qualification
- registered apprenticeship
- state-recognized trade credential
- manufacturer-specific equipment certification

## 3. Approved Structural Direction

Pass 1 proposed:

- 15 units
- 5 lessons per unit
- 75 lessons total

Pass 2 preserves that structure.

The course may reuse or contextualize many universal and technical capabilities, but the manufacturing domain is broad enough to justify structured industry context across production, quality, automation, maintenance, clean manufacturing, and semiconductor environments.

## 4. Classification Model

Each lesson is classified as:

### TRANSFERABLE

The core learner capability is cross-industry and should preferentially reuse Universal Career Readiness.

### CONTEXTUALIZED

The core capability already exists but requires manufacturing-specific examples, workflows, risks, or terminology.

### INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC

The objective is genuinely manufacturing-specific and cannot be satisfied by changing examples alone.

Industry-specific does not mean regulated or employer-authorized.

## 5. Curriculum Composition Principle

Preferred composition:

Universal Career Readiness
→ manufacturing contextualization
→ technical awareness
→ manufacturing-specific source content only where necessary
→ evidence candidates
→ authorized review where applicable
→ Career transition
→ external technical qualification where required

Do not duplicate existing UCR, Data Center, electrical, cybersecurity, HVAC, or other canonical technical capability where functional equivalence already exists.
