## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand biotechnology and pharmaceutical manufacturing workplaces, develop quality-minded professional habits, follow documentation and controlled-environment expectations, recognize safety and scope boundaries, and make informed next-step career decisions.

This course is readiness education.

It does not independently confer GMP qualification, laboratory authorization, cleanroom qualification, pharmaceutical manufacturing authorization, certification, licensure, or employer qualification.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences may include:

- secondary students
- youth workforce participants
- adult career changers
- learners exploring biotechnology
- learners exploring biomanufacturing
- learners exploring pharmaceutical manufacturing
- learners exploring laboratory careers
- learners exploring QA / QC roles
- learners preparing for employer or partner technical training

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Biotechnology & Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Readiness
→ Career / occupation-specific preparation
→ Employer / partner technical training
→ External credential or regulated qualification where applicable
→ Employment

This course owns the preparation/readiness layer only.

## 13. Safety Boundary

The course must distinguish:

Awareness
→ Readiness
→ Supervised Technical Training
→ Employer / Partner Qualification
→ Authorized Independent Work

SHU readiness content must not represent the learner as authorized to:

- handle biological agents
- culture cells or organisms
- handle hazardous chemicals
- prepare pharmaceuticals
- compound medications
- perform sterile processing
- perform aseptic processing
- enter regulated cleanrooms
- operate pharmaceutical production equipment
- release product
- approve GMP records
- perform clinical laboratory testing

## 14. GMP and Regulatory Boundary
