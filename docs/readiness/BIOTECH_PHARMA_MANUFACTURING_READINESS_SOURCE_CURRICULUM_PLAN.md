## 2. Course Intent

Biotechnology & Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Readiness prepares learners to understand:

- biotechnology manufacturing workplaces
- pharmaceutical manufacturing workplaces
- regulated-work expectations
- safety and scope
- quality culture
- documentation discipline
- data integrity
- GMP awareness
- controlled environments
- cleanroom concepts
- contamination-control awareness
- aseptic concepts
- material and sample traceability
- batch-record concepts
- QA / QC awareness
- manufacturing-process awareness
- validation
- controlled change
- deviation awareness
- biotechnology and pharmaceutical careers

The course is preparation and awareness.

It is not professional qualification.

## 3. Course Boundary

The course must not independently represent learners as qualified to:

- handle hazardous chemicals
- handle regulated biological materials
- handle clinical specimens
- perform sterile processing
- perform aseptic processing
- enter regulated cleanrooms
- operate pharmaceutical production equipment
- approve GMP records
- release pharmaceutical batches
- perform clinical laboratory testing
- make regulated quality decisions
- act as qualified QA or QC personnel

Those permissions belong to external training, employer, safety, quality, regulatory, laboratory, or credential authorities.
