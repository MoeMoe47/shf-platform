# Business & Financial Operations Readiness — Course Blueprint

Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1
Branch: agent/readiness-business
Baseline: 518dda1
Stable key: `shu-business-financial-operations-readiness`

## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand day-to-day business operations and financial workflows; develop accurate documentation, reconciliation, customer-service, control, and decision-making habits; recognize legal, tax, banking, accounting, procurement, and payment boundaries; and make informed next-step career decisions.

This course is readiness education.

It does not independently confer accounting authority, tax-preparer authority, banking authority, procurement authority, payment authority, bookkeeping certification, professional licensure, or employer qualification.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences may include:

- secondary students
- youth workforce participants
- adult career changers
- learners exploring office administration
- learners exploring bookkeeping
- learners exploring banking
- learners exploring accounting support
- learners exploring procurement
- learners exploring sales operations
- learners exploring entrepreneurship
- learners preparing for employer or postsecondary business training

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Business & Financial Operations Readiness
→ Career / occupation-specific preparation
→ Employer / technical / postsecondary training
→ External credential, authorization, or qualification where applicable
→ Employment

This course owns the readiness layer only.

## 18. Initial Proof Activity Candidates

### Invoice Review Exercise

Learner reviews a simulated invoice and identifies:

- vendor
- invoice number
- date
- amount
- purchase reference
- discrepancy
- approval requirement
- safe next step

Potential measures:
- documentation accuracy
- exception detection
- authorization awareness
- escalation

### AP / AR Workflow Scenario

Learner classifies a simulated transaction and determines:

- whether it is payable or receivable
- required records
- status
- next workflow step
- unresolved questions

### Reconciliation Exercise

Learner compares two synthetic records and identifies:

- matching items
- missing item
- amount mismatch
- timing difference
- evidence needed
- escalation

### Procurement Controls Scenario

Learner reviews a simulated purchase request and identifies:

- requester
- approver
- vendor
- documentation
- conflict risk
- separation-of-duties concern
- appropriate next step

### Payroll Discrepancy Review

Learner reviews a synthetic pay record and identifies:

- observed issue
- relevant evidence
- sensitive-data boundary
- appropriate payroll / HR escalation

These remain candidates until competency and proof-configuration audits are complete.

## Final Blueprint Decision

Business & Financial Operations Readiness should teach business-process literacy and financial-operations readiness while preserving the authority of the systems it explains.

Canonical direction:

Universal Career Readiness
→ Business & Financial Operations contextualization
→ safe business and financial simulation
→ governed learner evidence
→ career connection
