Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1
Branch: agent/readiness-business
Baseline: 518dda1
Stable key: `shu-business-financial-operations-readiness`

## Purpose

Determine what SHU already contains for Business & Financial Operations Readiness before creating curriculum, services, registries, schemas, competencies, or authorities.

Required rule:

Audit → Identify Canonical Authority → Reuse → Extend → Consolidate Where Necessary → Build New Only When Proven Missing.

## Executive Finding

SHU already contains substantial business, finance, payroll, budgeting, Treasury, Credit, Debt, Student Enterprise, commercial-product, payment-readiness, procurement, and Metaverse commerce capabilities.

These systems are intentionally separated by authority.

This branch must not combine them into a new general financial authority.

The correct goal is a readiness course that teaches business and financial operations concepts while consuming existing canonical systems where appropriate.

## AUDIT DECISION

Business & Financial Operations Readiness is viable as an SHU readiness course.

The correct architecture is:

Universal Career Readiness
→ Business & Financial Operations contextualization
→ business-process and financial-operations simulation
→ governed learner evidence
→ career connection
→ employer / practitioner validation
→ external accounting, banking, tax, procurement, or business qualification where applicable

The course should teach operational literacy without becoming the authority for the financial systems it explains.

Proceed to blueprint only after preserving these boundaries.
