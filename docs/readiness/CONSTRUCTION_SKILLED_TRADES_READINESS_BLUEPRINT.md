# Construction & Skilled Trades Readiness — Course Blueprint

Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1
Branch: agent/readiness-construction
Baseline: 518dda1
Stable key: `shu-construction-skilled-trades-readiness`

## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand construction and skilled-trades workplaces, develop jobsite-ready professional habits, recognize safety and scope boundaries, interpret basic plans and work instructions, understand major trade families, and prepare for employer, apprenticeship, or partner technical training.

This course is readiness education.

It does not independently confer licensure, apprenticeship status, trade certification, union membership, or employer authorization.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences may include:

- secondary students
- youth workforce participants
- adult career changers
- pre-apprenticeship learners
- learners exploring electrical trades
- learners exploring HVAC
- learners exploring welding
- learners exploring carpentry
- learners exploring plumbing
- learners exploring construction technology
- learners preparing for work-based learning or apprenticeship pathways

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Construction & Skilled Trades Readiness
→ Trade-family preparation
→ Employer / apprenticeship / partner technical training
→ Credential / licensure / apprenticeship where applicable
→ Employment

This course owns the preparation/readiness layer only.

## 8. Initial Proof Activity Candidates

### Jobsite Hazard and Escalation Scenario

Learner reviews a simulated jobsite and identifies:

- hazards
- restricted activities
- appropriate stop-work response
- who should be notified
- what should be documented

Potential measures:
- hazard recognition
- safety judgment
- communication
- escalation

### Blueprint Interpretation Exercise

Learner interprets a simplified construction drawing and identifies:

- dimensions
- symbols
- relevant notes
- uncertainties
- verification questions

Potential measures:
- reading accuracy
- measurement reasoning
- attention to detail
- appropriate verification

### Trade Coordination Scenario

Learner receives a multi-trade sequencing problem and explains:

- dependencies
- handoffs
- likely conflict
- communication plan
- escalation path

Potential measures:
- teamwork
- systems thinking
- communication
- documentation

### Apprenticeship Readiness Plan

Learner researches a real pathway and distinguishes:

- preparation
- eligibility
- application
- formal training
- OJT
- external credential or licensure

Potential measures:
- career research
- truthful interpretation
- next-step planning
- no overclaiming

These remain candidates until competency and proof-configuration audits are complete.

## 14. Safety Boundary

The course must distinguish:

Observe
→ Understand
→ Simulate
→ Supervised Practice
→ Formal Training / Apprenticeship
→ Authorized Independent Work

SHU readiness content must not represent the learner as authorized to:

- perform live electrical work
- work on energized systems
- perform HVAC service
- perform gas work
- weld independently
- work at height
- enter confined spaces
- operate heavy equipment
- perform excavation
- perform lockout/tagout independently
- perform licensed trade work
