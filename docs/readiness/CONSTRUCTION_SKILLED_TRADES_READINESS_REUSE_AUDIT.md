# Construction & Skilled Trades Readiness — Reuse Audit

Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1

## Purpose

Determine what SHU already contains for Construction & Skilled Trades Readiness before creating curriculum, services, registries, schemas, competencies, or authorities.

Required rule:

Audit → Identify Canonical Authority → Reuse → Extend → Build New Only When Proven Missing.

## Executive Finding

SHU already contains substantial Skilled Trades pathway concepts and reusable technical-readiness infrastructure, especially in electrical and mechanical/HVAC domains.

Existing repository evidence includes:

- Electrician Apprentice pathway concepts
- HVAC Technician Trainee pathway concepts
- Construction Tech (Site & Drones)
- Welding (MIG/TIG)
- OSHA-related fixture references
- blueprint-reading references
- electrical safety readiness
- mechanical/HVAC safety readiness
- existing electrical and mechanical/HVAC Prepare/Prove domains
- existing electrical and mechanical/HVAC proof activities
- Career Pathway Acceptance Standard
- apprenticeship/workforce reviewer patterns
- employer/training validation governance

The audit did not prove an existing governed Construction & Skilled Trades Readiness course.

They do not independently establish:

### Electrical and HVAC Readiness Content

Classification: REUSE / EXTEND

Existing Data Center readiness infrastructure already contains:

- electrical-infrastructure specialization
- mechanical-HVAC specialization
- electrical reasoning proof activities
- cooling/HVAC reasoning proof activities
- simulation-only safety boundaries
- escalation requirements
- evidence-based technical reasoning

Construction should reuse the pattern and any functionally equivalent content rather than duplicate it.

Construction-specific content may extend beyond Data Center context.

### Career Pathway Acceptance Standard

Classification: KEEP — CANONICAL

Reuse the existing distinction between:

Explore
→ Prepare
→ External Validation
→ Work-Based Learning / Apprenticeship
→ Credential / Licensure
→ Employment

SHF completion must not be represented as automatically conferring apprenticeship, credential, licensure, or employment status.

### Apprenticeship / Workforce Review Pattern

Classification: REUSE

Existing Data Center employer-validation work already distinguishes:

- employer/operator reviewer
- apprenticeship/workforce reviewer
- training/education reviewer

Reuse this governance model for Construction & Skilled Trades.

Do not treat an apprenticeship body as a substitute for employer validation unless canonical policy explicitly allows it.

## Safety Boundary

The course may teach:

- hazard recognition
- PPE awareness
- stop-work decisions
- site awareness
- tool identification
- measurement reasoning
- blueprint interpretation
- safe communication
- escalation
- verification
- observation
- simulation

The course must not independently authorize learners to:

- perform live electrical work
- service energized systems
- perform HVAC service
- perform gas work
- operate dangerous construction machinery
- weld independently
- work at height
- perform excavation
- perform confined-space entry
- perform lockout/tagout independently
- perform licensed trade work

Those permissions belong to employer, apprenticeship, training, safety, or regulatory authorities.

## Apprenticeship Boundary

Course completion does not establish:

- apprenticeship admission
- apprenticeship sponsorship
- apprenticeship standing
- journeyman status
- union membership
- employer placement

Those facts must come from their owning authorities.
