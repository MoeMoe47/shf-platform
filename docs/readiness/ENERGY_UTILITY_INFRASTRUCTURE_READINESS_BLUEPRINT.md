# Electrical, Energy & Utility Infrastructure Readiness — Course Blueprint

Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1
Branch: agent/readiness-energy
Baseline: 518dda1
Stable key: `shu-energy-utility-infrastructure-readiness`

## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand electrical, energy, utility, and public-works workplaces; develop safe systems-thinking habits; interpret infrastructure concepts and simulated operational evidence; understand renewable and utility pathways; and make informed next-step career decisions.

This course is readiness education.

It does not independently confer electrical licensure, utility operating authority, lineworker qualification, SCADA authority, water-treatment authority, apprenticeship status, certification, or employer authorization.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences may include:

- secondary students
- youth workforce participants
- adult career changers
- pre-apprenticeship learners
- learners exploring electrical infrastructure
- learners exploring utility operations
- learners exploring solar and wind careers
- learners exploring lineworker pathways
- learners exploring public works
- learners exploring water and wastewater careers
- learners preparing for employer or partner technical training

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Electrical, Energy & Utility Infrastructure Readiness
→ Career / occupation-specific preparation
→ Employer / utility / apprenticeship technical training
→ External credential, licensure, or authorization where applicable
→ Employment

This course owns the preparation/readiness layer only.

## 14. Electrical Safety Boundary

The course must preserve the existing distinction between:

Observe
→ Understand
→ Simulate
→ Supervised Technical Training
→ Authorized Professional Work

SHU readiness content must not represent learners as authorized to:

- open energized panels
- probe mains voltage
- operate switchgear
- reset breakers
- service UPS systems
- operate generators
- work on live circuits
- execute professional lockout/tagout
- switch substations
- work on transmission lines
- work on distribution lines
- perform licensed electrical work
