## Electrical Safety Boundary

Existing SHU electrical safety boundaries remain controlling.

Learners may:

- interpret diagrams
- use synthetic telemetry
- perform mathematical reasoning
- analyze simulated power paths
- identify hazards
- document evidence
- recommend escalation

Learners must not be represented as authorized to:

- open energized panels
- probe mains voltage
- operate switchgear
- reset breakers
- service UPS systems
- operate generators
- perform live wiring
- execute professional lockout/tagout
- perform substation switching
- work on transmission or distribution lines
- perform licensed electrical work
