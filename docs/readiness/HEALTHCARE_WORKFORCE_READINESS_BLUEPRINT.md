## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand healthcare workplaces, develop healthcare-contextualized professional readiness, practice safe non-clinical decision making, communicate effectively, understand role and scope boundaries, build evidence of transferable readiness, and make informed next-step career decisions.

This course does not replace state-approved, employer-approved, credential-specific, or clinical training.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences include:

- youth career explorers
- secondary students
- workforce-preparation participants
- adult career changers
- pre-STNA learners
- pre-phlebotomy learners
- learners exploring nursing and allied-health pathways

The course may also serve as an orientation layer before partner-delivered regulated training.

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Healthcare Workforce Readiness
→ Career / occupation-specific preparation
→ External regulated training where required
→ Credential / licensure authority
→ Employment

Healthcare Workforce Readiness owns the preparation/readiness layer only.

## 8. Initial Proof Activity Candidates

### Healthcare Communication and Handoff

Learner receives a non-clinical scenario and produces a concise, accurate handoff.

Potential measures:

- clarity
- completeness
- role-appropriate communication
- appropriate escalation

### Safety / Scope Decision

Learner identifies a situation outside their role or authorization and explains the appropriate stop, escalate, report, or verify response.

Potential measures:

- hazard recognition
- scope awareness
- escalation
- verification

### Evidence and Career Self-Advocacy

Learner presents readiness evidence without claiming credentials or clinical authority they do not possess.

Potential measures:

- truthful representation
- evidence selection
- career communication
- avoiding overclaiming

These remain candidates until competency and proof-configuration audits are complete.

Proof submission alone must not create verified competency or completion.

## 13. Regulatory Boundary

The course must distinguish:

Explore
→ Prepare
→ Readiness
→ Pre-Clinical / Pre-Certification
→ State- or Partner-Approved Training
→ Credential / Licensure
→ Employment

SHU Healthcare Workforce Readiness currently owns Explore / Prepare / Readiness.

External authority is required for regulated training, credentialing, licensure, scope of practice, and clinical placement where applicable.

## 14. Completion Boundary

Course completion does not establish:

- STNA eligibility
- nursing licensure
- phlebotomy certification
- EMT / paramedic certification
- clinical competency
- employer authorization
- job placement

Those facts must come from their owning authorities.
