## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand logistics and supply-chain workplaces, follow safe and reliable operational practices, manage information and handoffs accurately, understand inventory and freight flow, respond to disruptions, and make informed next-step career decisions.

This course is readiness education.

It does not independently confer equipment certification, commercial-driver qualification, port access, hazardous-material authorization, or employer qualification.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences may include:

- secondary students
- youth workforce participants
- adult career changers
- learners exploring warehousing
- learners exploring distribution
- learners exploring supply chain
- learners exploring freight and transportation
- learners exploring inventory roles
- learners exploring port and intermodal logistics
- learners preparing for employer or partner technical training

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Logistics & Supply Chain Readiness
→ Career / occupation-specific preparation
→ Employer / partner technical training
→ External certification or licensing where applicable
→ Employment

This course owns the preparation/readiness layer only.

## 8. Initial Proof Activity Candidates

### Receiving Discrepancy Exercise

Learner compares a simulated shipment record with received items and identifies:

- quantity mismatch
- condition issue
- documented facts
- next verification step
- escalation path

Potential measures:
- accuracy
- documentation
- verification
- escalation

### Inventory Traceability Exercise

Learner follows a simulated item through:

- receipt
- storage
- location
- status
- pick
- shipment

Potential measures:
- traceability
- record accuracy
- sequencing
- attention to detail

### Supply Chain Disruption Scenario

Learner receives a late-supplier or blocked-route scenario and identifies:

- affected dependencies
- known facts
- assumptions
- options
- communication plan
- escalation

Potential measures:
- systems thinking
- problem solving
- communication
- uncertainty management

### Logistics Handoff Exercise

Learner prepares a concise shift or shipment handoff containing:

- current status
- exceptions
- risks
- unfinished work
- next steps

Potential measures:
- clarity
- completeness
- accuracy
- role-appropriate communication

These remain candidates until competency and proof-configuration audits are complete.

## 13. Safety Boundary

The course must distinguish:

Observe
→ Understand
→ Simulate
→ Supervised Training
→ Employer Authorization
→ Independent Operation

SHU readiness content must not represent the learner as authorized to:

- operate forklifts
- operate cranes
- operate container-handling equipment
- operate warehouse machinery
- drive commercial vehicles
- enter restricted port areas
- handle hazardous materials
- perform regulated transport duties

## 14. Completion Boundary

Course completion does not establish:

- forklift certification
- CDL status
- hazardous-material endorsement
- port access credential
- equipment authorization
- employer qualification
- guaranteed placement

Those facts must come from their owning authorities.
