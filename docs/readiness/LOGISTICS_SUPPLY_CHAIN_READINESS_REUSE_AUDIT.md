# Logistics & Supply Chain Readiness — Reuse Audit

Status: DRAFT — PARALLEL PASS 1

## Purpose

Determine what SHU already contains for Logistics & Supply Chain Readiness before creating curriculum, services, registries, schemas, competencies, or authorities.

Required rule:

Audit → Identify Canonical Authority → Reuse → Extend → Build New Only When Proven Missing.

## Executive Finding

SHU already contains Logistics Coordinator pathway concepts, transferable supply-chain references, inventory and receiving workflows, and substantial Metaverse freight/port infrastructure.

The audit did not prove an existing governed Logistics & Supply Chain Readiness course or authoritative live logistics operations system.

Existing repository evidence includes:

- Logistics Coordinator pathway
- Supply Chain Basics reference
- logistics employer classification
- inventory and asset-record practices
- receiving and deployment workflow concepts
- communication and handoff curriculum
- Shipping Corridor
- Harbor / Port Entrance
- Container Yard
- Freight Highway
- cargo ship registry
- MOCC Port / Logistics overlays

Metaverse port and marine operations are currently provisional spatial context only.

## Safety Boundary

The course may teach:

- warehouse awareness
- shipping and receiving concepts
- inventory reasoning
- route and flow analysis
- material-handling awareness
- hazard recognition
- documentation
- communication
- escalation
- exception handling
- simulation

The course must not independently authorize learners to:

- operate forklifts
- operate cranes
- operate container-handling equipment
- drive commercial vehicles
- enter restricted port areas
- operate warehouse machinery
- perform hazardous-material handling
- perform regulated transport duties

Those permissions belong to employer, training, safety, licensing, or regulatory authorities.

## Career Boundary

Course completion does not establish:

- forklift certification
- CDL status
- hazardous-material endorsement
- warehouse-equipment authorization
- port credential
- employer qualification
- job placement

Those facts must come from their owning authorities.
