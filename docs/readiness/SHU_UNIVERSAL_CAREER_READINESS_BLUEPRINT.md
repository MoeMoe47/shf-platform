## Purpose

SHU Universal Career Readiness is the shared transferable readiness layer used across Silicon Heartland workforce pathways.

It prepares learners to enter, function in, and transition within modern workplaces regardless of industry. It does not replace industry readiness, career-specific readiness, technical preparation, external training, credentialing, apprenticeship, or employer qualification.

## Canonical Progression

Explore → Universal Career Readiness → Industry Readiness → Career-Specific Readiness → Technical Preparation → Simulation / Demonstration → Verified Evidence → External Training / Certification / Apprenticeship → Employment

## Course Boundary

Universal Career Readiness owns transferable workplace competencies. It does not own occupation-specific technical training, regulated training, employer qualification, hiring eligibility, credential issuance, learner evidence verification, career authority, portfolio authority, or student readiness scoring.

## Fifteen-Unit Course Structure

1. Entering the World of Work — workplace expectations, reliability, attendance, punctuality, conduct, responsibility, first-30-days planning. BUILD / CONSOLIDATE.
2. Professionalism — identity, conduct, accountability, boundaries, dependability, judgment. CONSOLIDATE + BUILD.
3. Workplace Communication — listening, verbal/written communication, professional messages, escalation, handoffs. REUSE / GENERALIZE.
4. Teamwork & Collaboration — team roles, cooperation, handoffs, feedback, accountability. REUSE / GENERALIZE.
5. Workplace Safety Culture — hazard awareness, procedures, PPE awareness, escalation, emergency expectations, authority boundaries. CONSOLIDATE.
6. Problem Solving & Decision Making — define problem, gather facts, options, consequences, escalation, documentation. BUILD.
7. Time, Attendance & Responsibility — punctuality, attendance, scheduling, priorities, deadlines, communicating delays. BUILD.
8. Digital & AI Workplace Literacy — workplace systems, digital professionalism, responsible AI, verification, privacy, security, shadow-AI awareness. BUILD / REUSE.
9. Accessibility, Inclusion & Accommodations — inclusive behavior, disability awareness, accommodations, assistive technology. REUSE / GENERALIZE.
10. Ethics, Rights & Responsibilities — ethics, honesty, confidentiality, workplace rights, responsibilities, reporting concerns. CONSOLIDATE + BUILD.
11. Career Planning & Employer Expectations — career research, job descriptions, training requirements, culture, pathway decisions. REUSE / EXTEND.
12. Resume, Interview & Professional Presentation — resume, experience translation, interviewing, presentation, follow-up. EXTEND / BUILD.
13. Evidence, Skills & Portfolio — evidence literacy, artifacts, feedback, portfolio organization, skill self-advocacy. REUSE.
14. Financial Readiness for Work — paychecks, deductions, benefits basics, budgeting, emergency savings. LIGHT CORE / REFER DEEPER SYSTEM.
15. Career Transition Plan — next-step plan, training, credential, WBL, apprenticeship, employer outreach, application readiness. REUSE / CONSOLIDATE.

## CORE / ENHANCE / IMMERSE

CORE: browser-based curriculum and assessment.

ENHANCE: optional Learning Arcade practice.

IMMERSE: optional Metaverse simulations or Missions.

## Safety Boundary

Universal safety covers hazard awareness, procedure compliance, reporting, PPE awareness, emergency expectations, stop-work/escalation behavior, and authority boundaries.

Industry/career courses retain electrical, HVAC, fireground, clinical, hazmat, aviation, machine-specific, and credential-specific safety.

## Financial Readiness Boundary

Unit 14 covers employment-transition financial literacy only. It must not duplicate Financial Passport, Credit Builder, Treasury, Debt, or deeper financial capability systems.

## Employer / Practitioner Validation

Create a reusable validation package covering reliability, communication, conduct, teamwork, time management, problem solving, digital behavior, interview expectations, and workplace expectations.

Universal validation happens once at the transferable layer; industry validation remains separate.

## No Student Readiness Score

Do not create a numeric Student Career Readiness Score. Use verified competency/evidence and pathway status instead.

## Initial Production Strategy
