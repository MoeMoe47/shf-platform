## Unit Identity

Course: SHU Universal Career Readiness

Unit: Unit 1 — Entering the World of Work

Purpose: establish foundational behaviors and expectations before any industry-specific readiness pathway.

## Why Unit 1 Comes First

The reuse audit found several universal gaps or underdeveloped topics:
- attendance
- punctuality
- workplace conduct
- employer expectations
- time/responsibility expectations

These should be built once and reused across every SHU pathway.

## Career Connection

Each lesson should show the same universal behavior in multiple pathways.

Examples:
- Data Center: late shift handoff can affect facility operations.
- Healthcare: reliability and communication affect team continuity.
- Fire/Emergency: dependability and chain-of-command communication are essential.
- Creator/Media: production schedules depend on preparation and communication.
- Logistics: missed shifts and handoff failures affect downstream operations.

Career authority remains canonical.

## Employer Validation Package

Review for:
- realism of expectations
- reliability standards
- attendance/punctuality
- professionalism
- accountability
- first-30-days expectations
- escalation behavior

Suggested questions:
1. Are these expectations common across entry-level roles?
2. Is anything unrealistic or overstated?
3. What behaviors most often cause a new worker to struggle?
4. What behaviors distinguish a strong new worker?
5. Are the scenarios realistic?
6. What should be added or removed?
7. Would this be useful preparation before industry-specific training?

A review does not imply hiring, credentialing, or funding authority.
