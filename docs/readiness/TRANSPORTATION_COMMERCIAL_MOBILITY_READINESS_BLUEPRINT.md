## 1. Course Purpose

Prepare learners to understand transportation, commercial mobility, fleet, transit, rail, aviation, and roadway operations; develop safe planning and documentation habits; recognize licensing and regulatory boundaries; and make informed next-step career decisions.

This course is readiness education.

It does not independently confer a CDL, DOT medical qualification, pilot certification, aircraft-maintenance certification, transit-operating authority, rail-operating authority, commercial-driving authorization, or employer qualification.

## 2. Intended Audience

Primary audiences may include:

- secondary students
- youth workforce participants
- adult career changers
- learners exploring commercial driving
- learners exploring fleet operations
- learners exploring transit
- learners exploring rail
- learners exploring aviation
- learners exploring UAS / drone careers
- learners preparing for employer, technical-school, or regulated training

## 3. Architectural Position

Universal Career Readiness
→ Transportation & Commercial Mobility Readiness
→ Career / occupation-specific preparation
→ Employer / operator / technical training
→ External licensing, certification, medical qualification, or authorization where applicable
→ Employment

This course owns the preparation/readiness layer only.

## 9. Initial Proof Activity Candidates

### Synthetic Pre-Trip Review

Learner reviews a simulated vehicle condition record and identifies:

- visible concern
- safety implication
- documentation need
- stop / proceed / escalate decision
- uncertainty

Potential measures:
- observation
- safety judgment
- documentation
- scope awareness

### Dispatch Disruption Scenario

Learner reviews:

- route
- schedule
- delay
- traffic condition
- passenger / customer impact
- available alternatives

Learner produces:

- updated plan
- communication
- escalation
- documented reasoning

### Transit Accessibility Scenario

Learner reviews a simulated passenger mobility scenario involving accessibility needs and identifies:

- barrier
- available accommodation
- safe communication
- service responsibility
- escalation need

### Transportation Incident Handoff

Learner reviews a synthetic incident and produces:

- known facts
- timestamps
- affected people / assets
- immediate safety boundary
- responsible handoff
- unresolved uncertainty

These remain candidates until competency and proof-configuration audits are complete.
