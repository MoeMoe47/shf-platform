## Architectural Purpose

Batch 03 exists to govern runtime owner closure after owner-neutral onboarding so future activation readiness can be evaluated without creating a runtime monopoly.

It introduces a conceptual architecture for owner activation readiness, runtime-boundary evaluation, evidence trust envelopes, and neutral integration closure.

Its constitutional objective is to preserve governance before runtime, evidence before trust, neutral infrastructure, canonical ownership, and independent certification.

The business outcome is consistent evaluation of future providers and owners without locking SHS BOS into a marketplace, protocol, or runtime owner position.

The engineering outcome is stable ownership, boundary, interaction, and contract law before any path or implementation design.

The ecosystem outcome is preservation of replaceable integrations, independent ownership, and registry neutrality across SHS BOS ecosystem participants.
