# Package H Batch 03 EPL Evidence Registration Plan

Plan ID: ERP-1

ERP-1 uses the permanent EPL-1 standard and creates no new evidence system.

Namespaces: `EPL-1/PKG-H/B03/CVF-1`, `EPL-1/PKG-H/B03/ACS-1`, `EPL-1/PKG-H/B03/EM-1`, and `EPL-1/PKG-H/B03/ERP-1`.

Workflow: produce evidence, assign EPL-1 category, record owner and producer, record validator, hash and fingerprint, register chain-of-custody, bind to criterion, and submit for certification.

Evidence Authority owns Phase 4 evidence records under EPL-1. Evidence Governance Authority is registration authority.

Registration occurs before certification entry. Validation occurs before independent certification. Publication timing belongs to a future publication phase only.

Retention strategy: constitutional retention under EPL-1; supersession only through governance.
