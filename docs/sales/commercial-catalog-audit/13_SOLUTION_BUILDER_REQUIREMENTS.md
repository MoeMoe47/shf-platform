# Solution Builder Requirements

Future Solution Builder question: "What should I sell this customer?"

The engine cannot be built until these registry inputs exist:

- Buyer type and authoritative organization profile.
- Industry and regulatory context.
- Population served and age/grade constraints.
- Goals and needs, recorded as discovery facts with provenance.
- Geography and local policy/funding context.
- Program/product readiness and sellability.
- Funding compatibility, eligibility authority, approval authority, and payment authority separated.
- Budget and procurement model.
- Implementation capacity and support requirements.
- Existing entitlements/products and renewal state.
- Evidence/reporting requirements.
- Accessibility/accommodation requirements.
- Employer relationships and partner dependencies.
- Career pathways and curriculum dependencies.
- Delivery capacity, QA gates, ClientOps readiness, and support SLA.

Minimum blocking gaps:

- No canonical Commercial Product Registry.
- No approved pricing/margin model.
- No entitlement-to-product mapping.
- No buyer/product fit rules.
- No legal/entity/tax review workflow.
- No authority-safe funding eligibility adapter.

Recommendation: build a registry/query contract before any recommendation engine.
