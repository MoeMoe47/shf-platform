# Gaps and Blockers

Evidence-supported blockers to a production Commercial Product Registry:

- Missing canonical product authority.
- Missing product readiness states connected to authoritative sources.
- Missing pricing authority.
- Missing selling-entity and delivery-entity approvals.
- Missing legal/tax/regulatory/procurement/funding review workflow.
- Missing buyer mapping per product.
- Missing entitlement mapping from product to service access.
- Missing product versioning and provenance model.
- Missing margin model.
- Missing commission eligibility and leadership override fields.
- Missing renewal and expansion definitions.
- Missing support/SLA model.
- Missing implementation requirement model.
- Missing partner-required model.
- Missing funding provenance and eligibility/approval/payment separation.
- Missing sponsorship policy and brand/tax treatment.
- Missing duplicate prevention between Store cards, service catalog, docs, UI routes, and authoritative domains.
- Missing Solution Builder input contract.

Important non-blocker: production UI presence is not enough. A page, route, game, simulation, or demo card can be evidence, but it cannot become a sellable product without authority.
