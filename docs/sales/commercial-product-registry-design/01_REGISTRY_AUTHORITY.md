# Registry Authority

The Commercial Product Registry is the authoritative Sales-facing commercial representation of legitimate SHU offerings. It governs whether an offering may be represented as a commercial product record, how that record is classified for Sales consumption, and what source authority supports each material claim.

## Questions It Answers

- What is this commercial offering?
- What authoritative ecosystem capability does it represent?
- Who may sell it?
- Who delivers it?
- Who may buy it?
- What commercial form does it take?
- What readiness state is it in?
- Is it currently sellable?
- What dependencies must be satisfied?
- What review gates remain?
- What entitlements apply?
- What funding relationships exist?
- What pricing status exists?
- What margin or commission status exists?
- Is it renewable?
- Is it expandable?
- What source authority supports every material claim?

## Positive Authority

The registry may own:

- Commercial product identity.
- Commercial classification.
- Sales-facing readiness and sellability projection.
- Review gate state as a commercial control surface.
- Commercial form metadata.
- Buyer compatibility metadata.
- Selling and delivery entity metadata.
- Pointers to source authorities.
- Product relationship metadata.
- Pricing, margin, commission, renewal, expansion, implementation, support, social selling, funding, and entitlement statuses as bounded references.

## Explicit Non-Authority

The registry must not become source authority for:

- Organization or identity truth.
- Curriculum or program truth.
- Career pathways.
- Funding eligibility, approval, claims approval, or payment.
- Contracts.
- Evidence, Truth Spine, or Oracle assertions.
- Treasury or financial books.
- Delivery, QA, ClientOps, or support operations.
- Reporting truth.
- Credentials, certifications, licenses, or regulatory approvals.

## Fail-Closed Rules

- A Sales card may only exist when a registry record exists.
- Operational facts must reference authoritative source records.
- Verified claims require verified evidence references.
- `SELL_NOW` requires approved source authority, pricing authority, review gates, support readiness, entitlement mapping, and commercial governance.
- Demo cards, UI routes, fixtures, public pages, and draft docs may inform discovery but cannot create commercial authority.
