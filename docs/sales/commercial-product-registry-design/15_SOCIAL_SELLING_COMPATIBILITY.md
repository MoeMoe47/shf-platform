# Social Selling Compatibility

The Sales App includes Social Selling and Employee Advocacy. The registry may expose product-level metadata that controls whether and how an offering can be referenced publicly.

## Fields

- `approved_public_marketing`
- `approved_social_sharing`
- `social_campaign_tags`
- `approved_talking_points_reference`
- `sales_language_status`
- `brand_restrictions`
- `marketing_authority_reference`

Allowed sales language status:

- `NOT_DEFINED`
- `DRAFT`
- `APPROVED_INTERNAL_ONLY`
- `APPROVED_PUBLIC`
- `RESTRICTED`
- `BLOCKED`
- `UNKNOWN`

## Rules

- The registry does not create marketing content.
- Public sharing requires approved talking points or marketing authority.
- Regulated, sponsorship, funding, evidence, and credential claims require stricter language controls.
- Social engagement does not create sourced revenue, commission, or leadership production without future policy authority.
