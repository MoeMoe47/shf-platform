# Gate Reviewer Checklists

Every reviewer must confirm candidate/scope, current authority, evidence provenance, effective period, limitations, and stale/expiry trigger. `APPROVED` means only the named gate is satisfied for the referenced scope. `BLOCKED` means a reasoned impediment exists. `STALE` means previously relevant evidence/decision is no longer current. No gate decision changes readiness or sellability.

## COMMERCIAL_READINESS
Verify the complete offer definition, buyer/use scope, source-backed evidence, and accountable owner. Accept governance review record plus source/evidence references. Do not infer readiness from existence or strongest-candidate ranking. Approval is limited to commercial-definition review; block when required definition/evidence is absent; stale when the definition or source changes.

## LEGAL
Qualified legal authority verifies entity, contract, IP, sponsorship, claims, and applicable terms. Accept documented legal opinion/review tied to exact scope. Sales cannot approve or interpret legal sufficiency. Approval is only the reviewed scope; block for unresolved legal impediment; stale on changed terms/entity/law or stated expiry.

## TAX
Qualified tax authority reviews actual seller, charitable/commercial boundary, sponsorship treatment, and transaction structure. Accept attributable tax review. Sales or registry cannot determine tax treatment. Approval applies only to the reviewed structure; block unresolved issues; stale on structural or rule changes.

## REGULATORY
External regulator, approved training authority, or authorized partner verifies applicable license, credential, curriculum, and scope. Accept current external rule/approval/partner evidence. SHU/SHF/SHS cannot self-assert external approval. Approval is exact-scope only; block for absent required authority; stale on expiry, scope, or regulator change.

## PROCUREMENT
Verify applicable procurement requirements using buyer/jurisdiction records. Accept authoritative procurement policy or buyer-side determination. Sales cannot infer purchasing approval. Approval is buyer/scope-specific; block for unmet requirement; stale on policy or cycle change.

## FUNDING
Verify only a product-to-funding-program compatibility relationship. Accept published funding source or documented compatibility evidence. Reviewer cannot approve a customer's eligibility, award, reimbursement, or guarantee. Approval means compatibility only; block unsupported relationship; stale when program/source changes.

## DELIVERY_CAPACITY
Named operations owner confirms people, facilities, dates, and partner commitments for the defined scope. Accept capacity plan/commitment. Product existence or forecast is not capacity. Approval is scoped; block unsupported delivery; stale on staffing, partner, or schedule change.

## EVIDENCE
Evidence/Truth authority verifies each material claim and provenance. Accept canonical verified-evidence reference and verification state. Sales copy, demo data, and source existence are not proof. Approval covers only cited claims; block unsupported claims; stale when evidence expires or source changes.

## SUPPORT
Named support owner confirms support model, owner, escalation, and coverage. Accept approved service/support record. Do not invent tier or SLA. Approval is exact service scope; block absent owner/model; stale on support-plan or coverage change.

## PRICING
Approved pricing authority verifies the referenced pricing record and effective period. Accept authority-owned pricing reference. Do not invent amounts, discounts, or quotes. Approval means the external pricing record was reviewed, not a price calculation here; block absent authority; stale on expiry or policy change.

## MARGIN
Finance/cost authority verifies referenced cost/margin policy. Accept governed finance record. Do not compute or infer percentages. Approval means the referenced finance decision only; block absent cost authority; stale on cost/policy change.

## COMMISSION
Commission policy authority verifies metadata eligibility and policy reference. Accept current approved policy. Do not calculate rates, payouts, overrides, or recruiting revenue. Approval is policy-status only; block unsupported product eligibility; stale when policy changes.

## ENTITLEMENT
Service Catalog/Entitlements authority verifies required/optional IDs and current status references. Accept canonical IDs/status records. Do not duplicate definitions in the Product Registry. Approval means mappings are valid; block missing/invalid references; stale on entitlement revision.

## PARTNER
Partner/legal authority verifies identity, authorization, scope, term, and delivery responsibility. Accept approved/executed partner record. Do not infer legal structure from a partner label. Approval covers documented relationship only; block absent authorization; stale on expiry or scope change.
