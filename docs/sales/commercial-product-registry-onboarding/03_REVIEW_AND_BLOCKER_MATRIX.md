# Review and Blocker Matrix

## Disposition meanings

`READY_TO_REGISTER` satisfies the persistence minimums and can be created without approving its gates. `SOURCE_AUTHORITY_MISSING` means only discovery, demo, fixture, audit-gap, or NOT_FOUND evidence exists. `CONTRACT_INVALID` is reserved for a value that fails contract validation after explicit mapping. `REJECTED_DUPLICATE` prevents a second canonical identity. `BLOCKED` is reserved for a documented blocker that prohibits safe insertion; it does not imply a generic commercial denial.

Review requirements are additive and do not automatically block registration. In particular, an item may be registered in lifecycle REVIEW while its legal, regulatory, funding, evidence, pricing, support, or partner gate remains pending/unknown. Registration never means approval.

## Source and regulatory blockers

Candidates with no credible governed authority are excluded from apply. Their manifest records which authority or live record is missing and retains the audit reference explaining the gap. Healthcare, fire/public safety, aviation, and apprenticeship-related candidates require external authority review where appropriate. No SHU credential authority is inferred.

## Known overlap controls

- Store demo cards are not products; a demo row cannot duplicate a canonical domain or offering.
- Data Center curriculum, employer validation, simulation, and pathway surfaces remain distinct only where the audit defines separate candidates; references do not imply a bundle.
- Service Catalog is a canonical service/entitlement authority and is not duplicated as entitlement definitions.
- Reporting, evidence, Truth Spine, and Oracle remain separate authorities unless an explicit product relationship is sourced.
- Learning Arcade and Classic Arcade remain separate candidates because sponsorship/tournament surfaces are not proof of the institutional license.
- Design-2 contains no sourced product-to-product edges for this onboarding. The importer therefore adds no relationships.

## Sponsorship coverage

Audit sponsorship classes remain review metadata and do not inflate the 32 count: program, destination, workforce pathway, Learning Arcade, Classic Arcade, Metaverse district, event, scholarship, equipment, infrastructure, employer-branded mission, and community initiative. Legal/tax review remains required as flagged in the audit. SHNN/media sponsorship was NOT_FOUND and is not represented as an authoritative product.
