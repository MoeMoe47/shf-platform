# Pilot Candidate Selection

The test cohort contains six deterministic candidates from the reviewed 4B manifest. Selection covers different commercial domains and includes unresolved source authority and regulated-program constraints; no candidate was selected because it was already approved.

| Candidate | Family | Why selected | Process expectation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cc-001` Data Center & AI Infrastructure Curriculum Pathway | `DATA_CENTER` | Data Center pathway, joint seller review, external training authority and funding/evidence dependencies | Verify only that gate-specific reviewers can access the packet and record a test-only pending/blocking state; joint structure remains unresolved. |
| `cc-005` Educator Professional Development Bundle | `CORE_EDUCATION` | Core education candidate with unresolved source, unknown readiness, unknown entities, and legal review pending | Source stays unresolved without evidence; a test-only source reference can prove the resolution workflow without becoming source truth. |
| `cc-011` Healthcare Workforce Pathway Concepts | `HEALTHCARE_WORKFORCE` | Regulated pathway with external credential/training authority and partner dependency | Regulatory/partner review remains external; no credential, licensure, or approval claim is made. |
| `cc-019` Metaverse Access License | `METAVERSE` | Source-resolved pilot classification with legal, procurement, and evidence gates | Exercise pending decisions and retain other unknown gates. |
| `cc-025` Studio / Student Project Workspace | `INSTITUTIONAL_TECH_SHS` | Institutional technology offering with legal/evidence and delivery/support unknowns | Verify evidence and legal capabilities remain separate. |
| `cc-029` CivicSure / Government Program Assurance | `ASSURANCE_VERIFICATION_REPORTING` | Assurance/reporting domain with procurement, evidence, legal, and commercial-readiness gates | Verify assurance claims still reference external evidence and no regulatory/assurance approval is implied. |

The six retain their manifest states: readiness `YELLOW` for `cc-001`, `cc-019`, `cc-025`, and `cc-029`; `UNKNOWN` for `cc-005` and `cc-011`. Sellability remains `PILOT` for `cc-001`, `cc-019`, `cc-025`, and `cc-029`; `DEVELOP` for `cc-005`; and `NEEDS_REVIEW` for `cc-011`. The source state is unresolved for `cc-005` and `cc-011`, resolved in the 4B manifest for the other four. None of these states is upgraded by the pilot.
