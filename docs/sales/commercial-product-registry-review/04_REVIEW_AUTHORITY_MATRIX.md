# Review Authority Matrix

The generated [REVIEW_AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md](REVIEW_AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md) maps all 14 canonical gates to the accountable authority, acceptable evidence, prohibited approvers, provenance, and staleness expectations.

Sales is not the universal approver. Approval requires a gate-specific qualified authority and recorded reference, reviewer identity, decision time, scope, and freshness/expiry basis. `NOT_REQUIRED` also requires an authoritative reason. `UNKNOWN` remains unresolved and fails closed.
