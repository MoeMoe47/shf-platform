# Production Onboarding Criteria

A candidate may be considered for a separately authorized production onboarding decision only when all criteria below are evidenced:

1. A credible, current canonical source authority and record/version are verified.
2. Candidate identity and all values pass the current Product Registry contract.
3. Selling and delivery entities are known and their legal/charitable/partner boundaries are documented.
4. Every applicable legal, tax, regulatory, procurement, funding-compatibility, delivery, evidence, support, pricing, margin, entitlement, partner, commission, and commercial-readiness gate has an authorized disposition. No unresolved blocking/pending/unknown gate remains; `NOT_REQUIRED` is itself supported by authority.
5. External regulated-training, licensing, and credential authority remains external and is evidenced where applicable.
6. Funding is compatibility metadata only; no buyer eligibility or award claim is made.
7. Readiness and sellability remain exactly as audited unless a separate governed correction/review explicitly changes them.
8. Provenance is sufficient to defend each material record and claim; no demo, fixture, keyword, or mock record is relied upon.
9. Duplicate identity and relationship checks pass, and a human owner separately authorizes a production execution plan.

Eligibility is not a sellability state. Passing these criteria does not imply `SELL_NOW`; any readiness or sellability changes require their dedicated governed review. This phase does not create a production execution script or perform onboarding.
