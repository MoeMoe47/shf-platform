## Non-blocking notes and disposition

- NB-REQ-02: Add informative examples for composite/hosted origins later; retain the existing version-identification obligation.
- NB-REQ-06: Informative dynamic-capability examples would help; unknown completeness must stay unknown, as already stated in the accepted scope.
- NB-REQ-07: Later guidance can distinguish used dependencies from merely accessible tools and show selector manifests; avoid expanding into an unlimited supply-chain census.
- NB-REQ-10: Keep the distinction between disclosure and runtime prevention prominent. Control efficacy reserved by deferred 0011 is post-1.0 work.
- NB-REQ-12: Informative examples should prevent reading or as a guarantee of all three interventions. Escalation/control-effectiveness obligations remain outside this baseline.
- NB-REQ-14: Keep plan/evidence/result examples distinct. Retention and evidence-authenticity schemes belong in later explicitly governed work.
- NB-REQ-16: An informative provider-state glossary example would reduce label confusion; no vocabulary expansion is needed for 1.0.
