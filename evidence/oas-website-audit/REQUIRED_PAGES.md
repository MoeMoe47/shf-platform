# Definitive page/function classification

## EXISTING AND COMPLETE

**No complete final-release website page.** Five entry/bootstrap paths and basic shared layout/assets work, but complete components are not complete pages.

## EXISTING BUT INCOMPLETE

- /oas.html — overview/authority page; draft copy, absent release information and empty illustration.
- /oas-1.html — final-standard presentation; obsolete state and unavailable actual specification/schema access.
- /oas-control-domains.html — real eight-domain overview; no canonical requirement mapping or detail navigation.
- /oas-purpose-boundaries.html — orphaned domain detail; broken sections, missing requirements/schema/related content.
- /oas-risk-classification.html — real informative risk presentation; scope/authority labeling and implementation access gaps.

## EXISTING PLACEHOLDERS

There are no standalone placeholder routes; these are disabled surfaces within real pages: Conformance, Schemas, Implementation; footer Principles/News/Specification/Implementation Guides/Developer Resources/Use Cases/FAQ/Process/Public Comment/Version History/Contact/Privacy/Terms/Accessibility; full TOC; Read Full Specification; Download PDF; Machine-Readable Schema; Explore Implementation; homepage Why OAS illustration. Some disabled nav (Risk/Control Domains) already has an implemented destination and should not remain planned.

## BROKEN

- Purpose #standards-schemas and #related-domains do not exist.
- Purpose Next Domain: Capabilities & Resources routes to the overview.
- Artifact/currentness/schema access is missing; HTTP 200 HTML fallback must not be accepted as a standards response.
- Governance/Watch Overview/specification CTAs resolve technically but do not deliver the implied function.
- Mobile 320px reflow and automated contrast checks fail; these are usability defects, not missing page routes.

## MISSING AND REQUIRED

Required information surfaces may be sections of existing pages; **new filenames/routes are DECISION REQUIRED**, not automatically mandated:

1. Current final release and artifact access: exact human standard/catalog/currentness/schema links, version/date/checksums/source pins and immutable release identity.
2. Complete canonical requirements view: 14 IDs/text across all 8 domains, with released/informative boundaries.
3. Developer declaration/consumption guidance: schema 1.1.0, version pinning/currentness, provider attribution, NOT_EVALUATED, no certification/behavioral verification, empty profiles.
4. Governance/versioning/authority summary tracing approved founding/adoption/publication records and explaining prospective change/freeze behavior. Approved participation/contact/owner/legal policies remain DECISION REQUIRED.
5. Clear independent Registry/ATB/BOS relationship and no operational/certification overclaim. This can extend the existing authority section rather than create a new app.

## OPTIONAL/FUTURE

- Separate detail pages for the other seven domains (all eight domains/requirements still need complete launch coverage in the main view).
- Standalone About, Principles, Terminology, FAQ, Use Cases, News, adoption/implementer stories with real approved evidence.
- Printable PDF, overview video, fuller illustration and optional examples/SDKs.
- Approved contextual Registry/ATB hyperlinks and improved Home brand navigation.
- Participation form, future governance body and legal/privacy/terms/accessibility pages according to approved institutional decisions; no invented email, policy or certification process.

## NOT REQUIRED / INAPPROPRIATE

- Invented profiles (frozen profiles=[]), OAS registration/issuance/public-record backend, ATB scores/assessment/certification engine, or BOS execution authorization.
- A new normative standard, rewritten requirement IDs, changed declaration contract/checksums or a fabricated adopter/certification list.
