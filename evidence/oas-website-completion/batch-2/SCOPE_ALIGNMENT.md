# OAS Website Batch 2 — Scope Alignment

Status: COMPLETE FOR BATCH 2

The public standard presentation now aligns its canonical requirement and
domain content with the frozen OAS-1 release.

OAS remains the standards and interoperability authority.

The website does not claim that OAS itself:
- registers autonomous systems
- issues Registry records
- certifies systems
- independently assesses trust
- behaviorally evaluates agents
- operates autonomous agents
- orchestrates business execution

Authority separation remains:

OAS:
standards, declarations, schemas, structural validation, interoperability,
governance of the standard.

Autonomous Registry:
identity, registration, provenance, versioning, lifecycle, issuance and
public record.

Autonomous Trust Bureau:
independent assessment and trust authority.

SHS BOS:
operational and business authority.

Risk Classification remains informational unless and until a normative
release explicitly creates risk-tier obligations.
